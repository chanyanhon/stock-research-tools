# MAG7 Equity-Research MVP Data Sources

**Research date:** 2026-10-03
**Scope:** Free or low-cost primary-data sources for a weekly and earnings-season MAG7 research MVP. This is a source and terms assessment, not an integration design or purchase decision. Prices and plan limits are those published on the cited pages when reviewed and can change.

## Recommendation

Build the research record from primary disclosures first: SEC EDGAR filings and company investor-relations releases, presentations, and webcasts. Use SEC company facts for reported fundamentals and keep each figure attached to its filing/accession and filed date. Obtain the current company estimate figures from a third-party consensus feed only when a forward multiple is needed; mark those estimates as vendor consensus, not company guidance.

For an individual, non-commercial MVP, Alpha Vantage's free API key is the broadest single low-cost starting point for weekly adjusted prices, corporate-action records, quarterly/annual statement convenience, reported EPS history, estimate aggregates, and a transcript endpoint. Its free service is limited to 25 requests/day, and the full-history daily-adjusted endpoint is premium; weekly adjusted history is documented as 25+ years. The SEC remains the authority for reported numbers, and issuer IR remains the authority for guidance. Do not use free individual-use vendor terms to power a commercial or publicly distributed product.

If daily price history longer than the free Alpha Vantage daily window is essential, compare the documented individual-use tiers from FMP and Massive against the user's actual use case and terms. FMP Basic advertises 5 years of EOD history and 250 calls/day; Massive Stocks Basic advertises 2 years, EOD data, corporate actions, and 5 calls/minute. These are not commercial-use recommendations. No paid plan is selected here.

## Timing, History, and Revision Behavior

- **SEC freshness:** submissions JSON is updated as filings are disseminated, typically under one second; XBRL APIs typically under one minute, with longer peak-period delays. The public filing itself may take 1-3 minutes after EDGAR acceptance to appear on sec.gov; SEC says this lag is not guaranteed. [SEC API update schedule](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces#update-schedule), [SEC availability FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions#lag).
- **SEC as-of reconstruction:** filing records carry accession and filing metadata; XBRL facts include filing-related context. Preserve each source response and select filings with a filed/accepted timestamp no later than the research cutoff. Frames return the last-filed fact fitting a calendar frame, so they are convenient current views, not point-in-time history. SEC also documents post-acceptance corrections/removals; historical indexes may not reflect later corrections until rebuilt. [SEC API docs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), [EDGAR corrections](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data#post-acceptance-corrections-and-deletions).
- **SEC request limits:** maximum 10 requests/second; identify the client with a descriptive `User-Agent` including a contact and download only needed data. [SEC fair-access policy](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data#fair-access), [SEC developer FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions#code-support).
- **Price/action timing:** Alpha Vantage's standard quote is end-of-day; its daily adjusted feed is premium, while weekly adjusted history covers 25+ years. Massive action endpoints are updated daily. The reviewed vendor plan/docs do not promise a universal exact EOD publication clock or historical data-vintage API. Store retrieval time and source payload; do not assume today's adjusted series is the series users saw on a historical date. [AV quote docs](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/#latestprice), [AV time-series docs](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/#weeklyadj), [Massive dividend plan recency](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/dividends#plan-recency).
- **Revision risk:** SEC filings and accession history support point-in-time selection more robustly than vendor-normalized current financials. SEC post-acceptance corrections can alter what is currently served. The vendor materials reviewed describe estimate revisions at an aggregate level, but do not demonstrate a complete immutable, queryable as-of history for market bars, normalized fundamentals, or consensus estimates. Confirm with the selected vendor before any point-in-time analysis.

## Terms and Attribution: Human Review Required

1. **Decide the deployment boundary first.** FMP and Massive label the free tiers individual/personal-use. FMP expressly defines commercial use broadly and bars integration into tools/apps available to other people absent written authorization. Massive says an individual/personal account cannot be used for business/commercial purposes, and its business terms limit distribution and derived investment strategies unless licensed. [FMP terms](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service), [Massive individual terms](https://massive.com/legal/individuals-terms-of-service), [Massive business terms](https://massive.com/legal/businesses-terms-of-service).
2. **Do not equate a price/API key with data display rights.** FMP requires a specific agreement for display/redistribution. Massive business service grants defined access but restricts making Information available, redistribution, and derivatives, while third-party exchange/provider agreements can add terms. [FMP terms](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service), [Massive business terms](https://massive.com/legal/businesses-terms-of-service), [Massive market-data terms](https://massive.com/legal/market-data-terms-of-service).
3. **Alpha Vantage:** its public policy says exchange-regulated realtime and 15-minute delayed US data is personal/non-commercial through premium, and business use requires sales onboarding. The general free-key support page says most datasets get 25 requests/day. I did not establish a public blanket license for redistributing EOD price, estimate, transcript, or normalized fundamental payloads; obtain written confirmation for any third-party display, multi-user service, or retained dataset. [AV policy](https://www.alphavantage.co/realtime_data_policy/), [AV terms](https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/), [AV support](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/#support-faq).
4. **SEC and issuer material:** SEC says government-created and public EDGAR filing content is free to access and reuse; SEC rate limits and embedded third-party content still matter. Issuer IR pages are primary publication locations, not a blanket grant to republish full transcripts, presentations, or logos. Use links and short attributable quotations unless rights are confirmed. [SEC reuse FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions#reuse), [SEC fair access](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data#fair-access).
5. **Attribution:** cite filings/releases in every research output with company, form/document, period, filed/published date, and URL. Vendor pages reviewed do not establish that attribution alone permits redistribution. Confirm provider attribution text, storage/caching duration, derived data rules, exchange fees, end-user classification, and post-termination deletion obligations with counsel/provider.

## Gaps and Operational Guardrails

- **No primary analyst consensus exists.** Company guidance is not a substitute for street estimates. Without a licensed estimate dataset and timestamped snapshots, forward PE/PEG should be marked unavailable rather than inferred from a current estimate presented as historical knowledge.
- **PEG is method-dependent.** State whether the numerator uses current or forward P/E, which fiscal years define EPS growth, treatment of negative/near-zero EPS, and whether growth is annualized. Keep source estimate count and retrieval timestamp with every result.
- **Peer taxonomy is weak for free primary data.** Curate peer membership and explain segment overlap; do not treat a vendor sector label or SEC SIC as definitive peer equivalence.
- **Adjustment conventions differ.** Record raw close, split-adjusted close, dividend-adjusted close, and action events distinctly. Prefer raw bars plus declared split/dividend events when reproducing a historical calculation; preserve provider adjustment version/retrieval date.
- **Issuer transcripts are uneven.** Maintain a per-company availability check and label a vendor transcript as such. The endpoint's advertised history does not prove coverage for every MAG7 fiscal quarter.
- **Commercial terms are not resolved by this research.** Before deployment beyond one person's noncommercial research, obtain legal/provider confirmation for market-data display, analyst estimate use, transcript storage, redistribution, exchange reporting, and deleted/derived data handling.
- **No service-level guarantees were established** for vendor EOD delivery or correction windows from the public pages reviewed. Design the MVP to show source timestamps and tolerate missing/late updates.
